## Starch Betainate and Its Production

This invention describes the synthesis and application of starch betainate, a novel cationic starch derivative developed as a sustainable bio-based additive for papermaking. The invention provides an environmentally friendly modification route for starch using naturally derived reagents while improving the mechanical properties of paper.

I was responsible for:

- Designing and optimizing the synthesis route.
- Developing reaction conditions.
- Characterizing the modified starch using FTIR, NMR, and thermal analysis.
- Evaluating its performance in laboratory papermaking.
- Demonstrating improvements in paper strength and printability.
- Supporting intellectual property development and scientific dissemination.

Patent family
- WO2022168011 (International PCT)
- EP4289869 (European phase)
- PT117053 (Portugal)

Status: Granted in Portugal and Europe; Published international patent application

## Betaine Hydrochloride Formulations and Applications

This invention relates to formulations and applications based on betaine hydrochloride, extending the use of betaine-derived chemistry for functional bio-based materials and industrial applications.

I was responsible for:

- Development of the formulation.
- Experimental optimization.
