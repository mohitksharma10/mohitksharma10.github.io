# About


I am currently a Junior Researcher (Contract Researcher) at [Chemical Engineering and Renewable Resources for Sustainability; CRERS](https://www.uc.pt/ceres/), funded by the Foundation for Science and Technology (FCT) at Department of Chemical Engineering, University of Coimbra, Portugal. 

I began my scholar journey after receiving a competitive scholarship from the MHRD, Government of India (GOI), to pursue a Master of Technology degree at one of India’s leading institutions, the Department of Paper and Packaging Technology, Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee (2012–
2014). During my masters' studies, I ranked among the top students and was selected for the DAAD scholarship. This enabled me
to complete my master thesis as a visiting scholar at the TU Darmstadt, Germany, where my research focused on the development of novel photoreactive
polymers to improve the wet strength properties of paper.

I then transitioned to industry and was selected for a full-time public sector position as a Process Engineer at Nepa Limited, Asia’s oldest newsprint
paper manufacturing company, operating under the Government of India. From 2014 to 2018, I gained extensive hands-on industrial experience
across multiple departments, including paper machine operations, project engineering, and industrial safety.
Motivated by this industrial exposure and a strong interest in sustainability within the paper sector, I pursued doctoral studies in close collaboration
with the Portuguese paper industry, particularly The Navigator Company. From 2018 to 2023, I completed my PhD at UC, focusing on the
development of bio-based materials to enhance paper surface properties. Working in a multidisciplinary research environment, I developed a novel
transesterification route for corn starch functionalization and formulated sustainable coating systems based on modified starch, nanocellulose, and
rheology modifiers for paper-based applications.

During my PhD, I acquired strong research and professional skills, including scientific writing and intellectual property development. I authored five
peer-reviewed research articles as first author and contributed to two patented technologies with national, European, and international patent
filings. I also mentored visiting master and PhD students and actively contributed to collaborative research projects.
Towards the completion of my PhD, I became increasingly focused on sustainable paper-based packaging. This led me to join the PRR-FF2F project
in July 2023, where I worked on strengthening packaging-grade papers using microfibrillated cellulose under industrially relevant conditions. I
applied Six Sigma methodologies to industrial datasets, designed pilot-scale experiments that closely mimicked paper mill conditions, and identified
key challenges in papermaking wet chemistry. I proposed and validated bio-based solutions that enabled the industrial use of microfibrillated
cellulose for paper reinforcement. These findings were recently published as two first-author articles in the journal Cellulose.
From 2023 onward, I have continued my research career at CERES, mentoring international PhD students, collaborating with postdoctoral
researchers, and co-authoring four additional articles in high-impact journals. I have supervised a master’s thesis, served as a jury member for
another master thesis, and contributed to teaching at UC in the areas of pulp and paper science and nanotechnology.
Throughout my career, I have actively disseminated my research through oral and poster presentations at 13 international conferences. Most
recently, I became a winning research team member of a competitive seed funding program awarded under the 7th Edition of the Seed Projects for
Interdisciplinary Research, supported by Fundação Santander, Portugal. The €20,000 project, titled “Ligold” (2026–2027), involves a
multidisciplinary team at UC focused on using lignin-based materials to recover gold from electronic waste.

I am a Junior researcher (under fix-term contract, July 2022 - Current) at [Chemical Engineering and Renewable Resources for Sustainability; CRERS](https://www.uc.pt/ceres/), funded by the Foundation for Science and Technology (FCT) at Department of Chemical Engineering, University of Coimbra, Portugal. At [CRERS](https://www.uc.pt/ceres/), I am studying the possibility to replace plastics in food packaging using bio‑based polymers in my postdoc work under the From Fossil to Forest project, which is in collaboration with paper and food packaging industries [The Navigator Company](https://thenavigatorcompany.com/), [RAIZ](https://raiz-iifp.pt/#), [EMBA](https://www.emba.pt/pt), [Roclayer](https://roclayer.com/en/) and [Bolseira](https://www.abolseira.com/). I have developed strategies to enhance the reinforcing effect of Lignin‑containing microfibrillated cellulose (LMFC) under industrial conditions, significantly improving the mechanical performance of flexible packaging‑grade papers. These results were recently published in Cellulose Journal ([Article 1](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10570-025-06516-9) and [Article 2](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10570-025-06846-8), and our industrial partners are assessing industrial‑scale implementation. I am also investigating strategies to modify chitosan to expand its potential in food‑packaging applications. The combination of chitosan, LMFC, and other bio‑polymers shows strong potential to improve both strength and barrier properties of packaging‑grade paper, supporting circular‑economy principles and industrial feasibility.

I completed my PhD (Oct 2018 - March 2023) in Chemical Engineering, focusing on biorefineries and pulp and paper technologies within the industrial R&D project [Inpactus; nnovative Products and Technologies from Eucalyptus](https://www.uc.pt/spgi/dpa/projetos-cof-ue/inpactus/), co-financed by the European Union. My thesis, titled [Bio‑based coatings for improving paper printing and barrier properties](https://estudogeral.uc.pt/handle/10316/114397?mode=full), explored sustainable solutions for improvement of paper surface properties. 

Before joining [CRERS](https://www.uc.pt/ceres/), I worked for four years (2014‑18) in two pulp and paper industries in India namely [Satia Industries Limited](https://www.satiagroup.com/) and [Nepa Limited](https://www.nepamills.co.in/). In [Satia Industries Limited](https://www.satiagroup.com/, I worked for 2 months as Post Graduate Engineer Trainee in Chemical Recovery plant. There, I Participated in hands-on training focused on the operation and maintenance of the Chemical Recovery Plant and also gained foundational exposure to pulp production, chemical handling, Evaporators and Recovery Boiler. In [Nepa Limited](https://www.nepamills.co.in/), I worked for almost four years as shift Incharge in Papermachine (two years) and project (two years) departments as shieft incharge. I led a shift team of 12–14 personnel to ensure consistent newsprint paper production from recylced newprint and printing and writing papers. Then, I joined project team and played a key role in the [RMDP (revival and mill development plan)](https://heavyindustries.gov.in/en/nepa-limited), a project funded by Ministry of Heavy Industries, Governemnt of India to complete modernization of the present plant with an enhanced capacity to 100,000 tons per annum and producing Writing and Printing Paper in addition to Newsprint.  I contributed in this project by maintaining regular communication with suppliers for project progress reporting, conducted on-site technical inspections at supplier facilities to evaluate compatibility of new equipment. I also supervised daily installation progress of new equipment, collaborating closely with technical teams.by supporting the integration of new paper machine and deinking equipment through vendor coordination and early-stage engineering input. This experience still shapes the way I approach research and problem‑solving.

I completed my Bachelor of Technology in Biotechnology in 2012 from [Vellore Institute of Technology, Tamilnadu](https://vit.ac.in/) and my Master’s in Pulp and Paper Engineering in 2014 from the [Department of Paper and Packaging Technology](https://iitr.ac.in/Departments/Paper%20Technology%20Department/index.html) at Indian Institute of Technology, Roorkee. During my master’s program, I received a a highly competetive scholorship from [DAAD; German Academic Exchange Service](https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/scholarships/daad-scholarships/to) to conduct my master thesis work at the [Department of Macromolecular and Paper Chemistry](https://www.chemie.tu-darmstadt.de/map/index.en.jsp) at Technical University of Darmstadt, Germany. During my master’s thesis at TU Darmstadt, I investigated photo‑reactive polymers to improve the wet strength of paper.

I have published Eleven peer-reviewed articles and presented my research work at thirteen international conferences. I have co-supervised a Master’s student, served as a jury member for a Master’s defense,
and collaborated with international researchers and PhD students from Complutense University of Madrid, INIA–CSIC Madrid, Universitat de Girona, and Instituto de Tecnología Celulósica, Argentina. These collaborations leveraged our bio-based coating materials and CERES facilities to advance their research. Beyond research, I actively contribute to teaching at the University of Coimbra, delivering courses on Pulp and Paper Science, Nanotechnology, and Biorefinery, and providing laboratory training in refining, strength testing, microscopy, and advanced analytical tools. As a reviewer for high-impact journals, I support the quality of scientific publishing. Committed to bridging academia, industry, and society, I also engage in science communication initiatives such as the European Researchers’ Night, presenting our bio-based packaging research to the public.

When I'm not doing research, I enjoy being active outdoors and spending time with my toddler. I love travelling, practicing yoga and playing cricket.
