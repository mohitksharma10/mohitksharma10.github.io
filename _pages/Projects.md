The core of my research has been dedicated to investigating sustainable bio-based materials, encompassing their synthesis, characterization, and functional evaluation for a wide range of advanced applications. I have involved in following Research and Development projects:

## InPaCTus Project

InPaCTus – Innovative Products and Technologies from Eucalyptus was one of Portugal's largest collaborative R&D initiatives in the forest-based bioeconomy. The project brought together The Navigator Company, RAIZ – Forest and Paper Research Institute, the University of Coimbra, and the University of Aveiro to develop innovative pulp, paper, tissue, biorefinery, and bio-based products from eucalyptus. The project aimed to strengthen Portugal's competitiveness in the pulp and paper sector while promoting circular economy and sustainable bio-based materials.

My PhD research at the University of Coimbra (2018–2023) was conducted within the framework of the InPaCTus project. Your work focused on developing sustainable additives and cellulose-based materials to improve the paper properties.

My contribution to the InPaCTus project can be summarized as follows:

- Developed the novel bio-based additive starch betainate for papermaking applications.
- Advanced sustainable chemical modification strategies for starch and other polysaccharides.
- Established relationships between polymer chemistry and paper performance.
- Demonstrated the potential of renewable additives to improve the strength and printability of paper products.
- Generated patents, scientific publications, and industrially relevant knowledge supporting the development of sustainable cellulose-based materials.

## From Fossil to Forest

From Fossil to Forest (FF2F) is a Portuguese PRR (Recovery and Resilience Plan) Innovation Pact led by Navigator Paper Setúbal and a consortium of 27 partners from industry and academia. The project's objective is to develop cellulose-based products and packaging solutions that replace fossil-plastic materials through six main work packages covering high-yield pulps, high-strength papers, barrier papers, biocomposites, smart paper sensors, and molded cellulose packaging.

I have been working on this project as a Junior Researcher at CERES, University of Coimbra, from July 2023 to July 2026, where your research is focused primarily on developing sustainable technologies to improve the mechanical strength and barrier performance of packaging-grade papers.

My contribution to the From Fossil to Forest project can be summarized as follows:

- Developed lignin-containing microfibrillated cellulose as a renewable reinforcement material for packaging papers.
- Demonstrated significant improvements in the mechanical performance of packaging-grade paper.
- Developed a chitosan-based strategy for white-water treatment and improved additive retention.
- Advanced knowledge of process-water chemistry and its influence on papermaking.
- Investigated renewable barrier coating concepts for sustainable packaging.
- Performed techno-economic assessments supporting industrial implementation.
- Produced scientific publications and conference contributions that support the project's goal of replacing fossil-plastic packaging with high-performance cellulose-based alternatives.
